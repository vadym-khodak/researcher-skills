# Summarize Academic Text

## Overview

Condenses a longer academic text to its essential points — thesis, key findings, methodology, and conclusions — without distorting the author's meaning.

## Workflow

1. **Identify target length** — ask if not specified: one sentence, one paragraph, one page, or bullet points
2. **Identify purpose** — reading notes, literature review entry, abstract replacement, or general overview
3. **Extract structure** — identify: research question, methodology, key findings, conclusions, limitations
4. **Write summary** — proportional coverage of each element; lead with the main claim
5. **Return** — summary at the requested length, plus a one-line note on what was omitted if significant

## Summary Formats by Purpose

| Purpose | Format | Length |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Literature review entry | 2–4 sentences: claim + method + finding + relevance | 50–80 words |
| Reading notes | Bullet list: RQ, method, findings, limitations | 5–8 bullets |
| Annotated bibliography | 3 sentences: what it argues, how, why it matters | 60–100 words |
| Executive overview | Structured paragraphs with subheadings | 200–400 words |

## What a Good Summary Covers

1. **Research question or thesis** — what problem does the work address?
2. **Methodology** — how was the research conducted? (briefly)
3. **Key findings** — what were the main results?
4. **Conclusions** — what do the authors claim the findings mean?
5. **Limitations** (if space allows) — what do the authors acknowledge as constraints?

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Copying sentences verbatim | Paraphrase all content; quote only if the exact phrasing is essential |
| Listing details instead of synthesizing | Lead with the main argument, support with key evidence |
| Adding your own interpretation | Summarize what the authors say, not what you think about it |
| Omitting the research question | Always state what the study was trying to find out |
| Disproportionate length on minor points | Weight coverage by importance, not by how much space it got in the original |
