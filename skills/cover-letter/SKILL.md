# Write a Submission Cover Letter

## Overview

Drafts a professional cover letter to accompany a manuscript submitted to a journal or conference. Concise, factual, and tailored to the target venue.

## Workflow

1. **Identify venue** — journal name, conference, or book series
2. **Gather manuscript details** — title, authors, word count, article type
3. **Check journal requirements** — some journals require specific statements (conflicts of interest, ethics approval, prior publication declaration)
4. **Draft letter** — following the structure below
5. **Return** — ready-to-send letter; flag any required statements the user must fill in

## Cover Letter Structure

```
[Date]

Dear Dr. / Professor [Editor's surname], / Dear Editorial Team,

We submit for your consideration our manuscript titled:
"[Full Title]"
for publication as a [article type] in [Journal Name].

[1–2 sentences: what the paper is about and its main contribution]

[1–2 sentences: why it fits this journal — scope, readership, recent related papers]

[Required declarations — use only what applies:]
• This manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration at any other journal.
• All authors have approved the manuscript and agree to its submission.
• The authors declare no conflicts of interest.
• Ethics approval was obtained from [institution] (approval no. [XX]).
• Data are available at [repository/upon request].

We look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,
[Corresponding author name]
[Affiliation]
[Email]
[ORCID if applicable]
```

## Journal Fit Statement

The most persuasive cover letters explain why *this* journal, not just any journal. Mention:
- The journal's stated scope that matches the paper
- A recent paper in the journal the submission builds on or contrasts with
- The target readership who will benefit

## Required Declarations Checklist

| Declaration | When required |
|-------------|--------------|
| Originality / no dual submission | Almost always |
| Author approval | Most journals |
| Conflict of interest | Most journals |
| Ethics approval | Human/animal subjects research |
| Funding acknowledgement | When funder requires it |
| Data availability | Growing requirement; check journal policy |
| Suggested reviewers | Some journals invite 2–3 suggestions |
| Excluded reviewers | Some journals allow listing competitors to exclude |

## Ukrainian-Specific Notes

| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| Українські журнали | Many Ukrainian journals do not require a cover letter — check the submission guidelines; if not required, a brief email to the editor suffices |
| Міжнародні журнали | Cover letters are standard for Scopus/WoS-indexed journals — always include one |
| Мова | Write in the journal's primary language; for bilingual Ukrainian/English journals, English is typically preferred for correspondence |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Summarising the abstract | The letter is not an abstract — focus on fit and contribution, not methodology details |
| Generic letter sent to every journal | Personalise the journal fit statement for each submission |
| Missing required declarations | Check the journal's author guidelines for mandatory statements |
| Addressing the wrong editor | Look up the current editor-in-chief; "Dear Editorial Team" is safe when unsure |
