# Research Methodology Advisor

## Overview

Helps choose and justify a research methodology that fits the research question, available resources, and disciplinary norms. Does not design the full methodology — helps select and frame the approach.

## Workflow

1. **Identify the research question** — ask user to state it (or use `research-question` skill first)
2. **Identify the goal** — explore, describe, explain, predict, or evaluate?
3. **Identify constraints** — time, sample access, data availability, ethical limits
4. **Recommend approach** — suggest methodology with rationale and trade-offs
5. **Suggest data collection methods** — matching the chosen approach
6. **Flag risks** — common pitfalls for the chosen design

## Methodology Selection Guide

| Research goal | Recommended approach | Why |
|---------------|---------------------|-----|
| Explore unknown territory | Qualitative (interviews, focus groups) | Generates concepts, not numbers |
| Describe a population | Quantitative (survey, census data) | Measures prevalence and distribution |
| Understand meaning/experience | Qualitative (phenomenology, grounded theory) | Captures lived experience |
| Test a relationship | Quantitative (correlation, regression) | Measures strength of association |
| Establish causation | Experimental / quasi-experimental | Controls for confounds |
| Both depth and breadth | Mixed methods | Triangulates findings |
| Synthesise existing research | Systematic review / meta-analysis | No primary data collection |

## Common Designs by Discipline

| Discipline | Common designs |
|------------|---------------|
| Business / marketing | Survey, case study, experiment, secondary data analysis |
| Social sciences | Survey, interview, ethnography, content analysis |
| Medicine / health | RCT, cohort, case-control, systematic review |
| Education | Action research, case study, quasi-experiment |
| Humanities | Textual analysis, historical, discourse analysis |
| Computer science | Design science, experiment, simulation |

## Data Collection Methods

| Method | Best for | Limitation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Online survey | Large samples, quantifiable attitudes | Low depth; response bias |
| Semi-structured interview | In-depth views; small sample | Time-intensive; not generalisable |
| Focus group | Group dynamics, shared meanings | Dominant voices skew results |
| Secondary data | Cost-efficient; large datasets | Limited to what was collected |
| Observation | Natural behaviour | Observer effect; access issues |
| Document/content analysis | Texts, media, policy | Availability of sources |
| Experiment | Causal claims | Artificial setting; ethical limits |

## Ukrainian-Specific Notes

| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| Методологія у вступі | Ukrainian theses require a methods section in the вступ: state методи дослідження as a bulleted list (e.g., аналіз, синтез, порівняння, анкетування) |
| Теоретична база | Cite Ukrainian and international scholars who established the theoretical foundation — both are expected |
| Емпірична база | State the source of empirical data explicitly: surveys, company data, statistical databases (Держстат, НБУ) |
| Наукова новизна | The methodology must support the claim of наукова новизна — ensure the design enables a genuinely new contribution |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Choosing methodology before the question | Always match method to question, not preference |
| Calling a survey "quantitative research" | A survey is a tool; quantitative is an approach — clarify both |
| Underestimating sample access | Check feasibility before committing to interviews or experiments |
| Mixed methods without integration | Mixed methods requires explicit integration of findings — not just "I did both" |
