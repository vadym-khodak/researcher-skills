# Respond to Peer Reviewer Comments

## Overview

Structures a professional, point-by-point response to reviewer comments that is respectful, thorough, and increases the chance of acceptance. Every comment gets a response — no exceptions.

## Workflow

1. **Categorise comments** — distinguish major revisions, minor revisions, and misunderstandings
2. **Plan responses** — decide: agree and revise, partially agree, or respectfully disagree (with evidence)
3. **Draft response letter** — structured format with point-by-point replies
4. **Draft manuscript changes** — note exactly what changed and where
5. **Cross-check** — verify every comment has a response and every response references a manuscript change

## Response Letter Structure

```
Dear Editor / Dear Reviewer [N],

Thank you for your thorough review of our manuscript [title].
We have carefully addressed all comments below.
Changes are highlighted in the revised manuscript.

---

REVIEWER 1

Comment 1.1: [paste reviewer's exact words]
Response: [your reply]
Manuscript change: [page/line reference or "no change — see explanation"]

Comment 1.2: …

---

REVIEWER 2
…

We believe the revised manuscript is significantly strengthened
by these revisions. We look forward to your further consideration.

Sincerely,
[Authors]
```

## Response Types

| Situation | Approach |
|-----------|----------|
| Reviewer is correct | "We agree. We have revised [X] on page Y." + show the new text |
| Reviewer is partly correct | "We partially agree. We have [done X] but maintained [Y] because [reason]." |
| Reviewer is mistaken | "We respectfully disagree. [Evidence/logic]. We have added clarification on page Y to prevent this misreading." |
| Reviewer asks for something out of scope | "This is an important question beyond the scope of the current study. We have acknowledged it as a direction for future research (page Y)." |
| Two reviewers contradict each other | Address both; explain the compromise you made; justify your choice |

## Tone Rules

- Always thank reviewers — even for harsh comments
- Never say a reviewer is wrong — say "we respectfully clarify" or "we have added text to address this concern"
- Be specific: cite page numbers, line numbers, or section names for every change
- Quote the new text when the change is significant

## Ukrainian-Specific Notes

| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| Мова відповіді | Respond in the language of the journal — English for international journals, Ukrainian for Ukrainian ones |
| Рецензування у ВАКівських журналах | Ukrainian national journals often use single-blind review; the response may go to the editor rather than directly to reviewers |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Ignoring any comment | Every single comment needs a response — skipping one signals carelessness |
| Vague responses ("We revised this section") | Always specify what changed, where, and show the new text |
| Arguing without evidence | If you disagree, cite literature or data — opinion alone won't persuade |
| Defensive tone | Reframe pushback as "clarification" or "additional detail added" |
| Not tracking changes in the manuscript | Submit a tracked-changes version alongside the clean version |
