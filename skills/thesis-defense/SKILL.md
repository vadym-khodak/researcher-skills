# Prepare for Thesis Defense

## Overview

Helps researchers prepare for an oral thesis defense: anticipates likely questions, structures answers, and reviews the presentation. Covers both content preparation and delivery.

## Workflow

1. **Identify defense type** — bachelor's, master's, PhD, or public viva
2. **Review the thesis** — ask for the title, research question, methodology, and key findings
3. **Generate likely questions** — by category (see below)
4. **Prepare answers** — draft concise, confident responses
5. **Review presentation** — check slide logic, timing, and opening/closing

## Likely Question Categories

### Research design questions
- Why did you choose this research question?
- Why this methodology and not [alternative]?
- What are the limitations of your design?
- How did you ensure validity / reliability?

### Findings questions
- What is your most significant finding?
- Were any results surprising?
- How do your results compare to [cited author]?
- What explains the discrepancy between X and Y?

### Contribution questions
- What is the original contribution of this work?
- What practical implications do your findings have?
- Who would benefit from this research?

### Future research questions
- What would you do differently if you started over?
- What are the next steps for this research area?

### Challenge questions (expect these at PhD level)
- Your sample is small — how do you justify generalisability?
- You only used one method — doesn't that limit your conclusions?
- [Author X] found the opposite — how do you reconcile this?

## Answer Structure (PREP)

Use PREP for concise, structured answers under pressure:

```
Point     — state your direct answer in one sentence
Reason    — explain why / the evidence
Example   — give a specific example from your thesis
Point     — restate or conclude
```

## Presentation Checklist

| Element | Check |
|---------|-------|
| Slide count | ~1–1.5 minutes per slide; 10–15 slides for a 15-minute presentation |
| Opening | State the problem and why it matters — not just the title |
| Findings slide | Most important slide — allocate extra time |
| Limitations | Address proactively — shows maturity |
| Closing | End with contribution and implications, not just "thank you" |
| Backup slides | Prepare extra slides for anticipated deep-dive questions |

## Ukrainian-Specific Notes

| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| Захист дипломної роботи | Standard format: 7–10 minute presentation + questions from the commission |
| Комісія | Typically 3–5 members; the scientific supervisor is present but cannot vote |
| Доповідь | Begin: `Шановна комісіє, дозвольте представити результати дипломної роботи на тему…` |
| Рецензент | The external reviewer (рецензент) submits written comments in advance — prepare responses to all rецензентські зауваження |
| Оцінювання | Commission evaluates: relevance, theoretical contribution, practical value, quality of presentation, and answers to questions |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Memorising a script | Learn the logic, not the words — scripts collapse under unexpected questions |
| Defensiveness about limitations | Acknowledge limitations confidently: "Yes, this is a limitation. Future research could address it by…" |
| Not knowing your own references | Be ready to explain any source you cited |
| Presenting methodology in too much detail | Committee knows methods — focus time on findings and contribution |
| Ignoring the rецензія | Every written comment from the reviewer must be addressed at the defense |
